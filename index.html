Welcome to Philips Feathers Poultry

We are a family-owned poultry farm dedicated to raising healthy chickens using sustainable and ethical farming practices. Our commitment to quality ensures that we deliver the freshest eggs and poultry products to our customers.

Free Range

Our chickens roam freely in open pastures, resulting in healthier birds and superior quality eggs.

Antibiotic-Free

We never use antibiotics or growth hormones, ensuring natural and healthy poultry products.

Fresh Delivery

We deliver fresh products directly to local markets, restaurants, and homes within 24 hours.
