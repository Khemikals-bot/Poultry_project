Poultry Farming Best Practices

At Philips Feathers, we follow strict guidelines to ensure the health and welfare of our chickens while maintaining sustainable farming practices.

Our Animal Welfare Standards

  1. Free-Range Environment: Our chickens have access to outdoor pastures for at least 8 hours daily.
  2. Natural Diet: We feed our chickens a balanced diet of organic grains, corn, and soybeans.
  3. No Antibiotics: We never use antibiotics preventatively, only when medically necessary under veterinary supervision.
  4. Stress Reduction: We maintain low stocking densities and provide environmental enrichment.
  5. Humane Processing: All processing follows strict animal welfare guidelines.
  6. Regular Health Checks: Each chicken receives weekly health inspections by trained staff.
  7. Natural Light Cycles: We mimic natural daylight patterns to support healthy circadian rhythms.

Sustainability Practices

  • Solar panels provide 40% of our farm's electricity needs
  • Rainwater harvesting system for animal drinking water
  • Composting of chicken manure for organic fertilizer
  • Use of biodegradable packaging materials
  • Zero-waste policy for farm operations
  • Native plant restoration around farm perimeters
  • Energy-efficient heating and cooling systems
