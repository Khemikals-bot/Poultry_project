About Our Farm

Philips Feathers Poultry Farm was established in 1980 by the Philipson family. What started as a small backyard operation with just 2 chickens has grown into a sustainable poultry farm with over 5,000 free-range chickens.

Our Mission

To provide the highest quality poultry products while maintaining ethical farming practices, ensuring animal welfare, and promoting sustainable agriculture.

Our Values

  • Animal Welfare: We prioritize the health and happiness of our chickens.
  • Sustainability: We implement eco-friendly practices to minimize our environmental impact.
  • Quality: We never compromise on the quality of our products.
  • Community: We support our local community through partnerships and employment.
  • Transparency: We are open about our farming practices and welcome visitors to our farm.

Farm History Timeline

  1. 1980: Farm established with 2 chickens in backyard operation
  2. 2005: Expanded to commercial scale with 500 chickens
  3. 2010: Implemented free-range farming practices
  4. 2015: Received organic certification
  5. 2020: Expanded facilities to accommodate 5,000 chickens
  6. 2023: Launched direct-to-consumer delivery service
  7. 2025: Received an international award for being the best farmer
